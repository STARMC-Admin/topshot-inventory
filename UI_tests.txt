TEST SUITE 1: WORKER SCAN & DISPATCH (Tab: "Worker: Scan & Dispatch")
================================================================================

--- TEST CASE 1.1: Standard Dispatch (Happy Path, Discount <= 20%) ---
Goal: Dispatch a physical racket with a standard discount without requiring admin PIN.
Steps:
  1. Open http://localhost:3000 and select tab: "Worker: Scan & Dispatch"
  2. In "Step 1: Physical Barcode", paste the barcode:
       TS-AERO-01-MV6ZH6A0-2PS6
  3. Verify that the Racket Details card appears (SKU: Topshot Aero Carbon 16mm Pro, Cost: ₹4,499, Status: In-Stock).
  4. In "Step 2: Delhivery Order Details", paste an unused tracking waybill:
       DLHV-TEST-1001
  5. Click "Verify Delhivery". Confirm buyer details load (Aarav Sharma / Mumbai).
  6. In "Step 3: Pricing & Approvals", leave Discount % at:
       10
  7. Notice that NO Admin PIN field is required because discount (10%) <= 20%.
  8. Click "Confirm & Dispatch Order".
Expected Result:
  - Green Success alert appears: "Order Dispatched Successfully!"
  - Generates unique Order ID (e.g., ORD-2026-...)
  - Barcode TS-AERO-01-MV6ZH6A0-2PS6 is atomically marked "Sold" in inventory.
  - Form resets and is ready for the next racket.

  3. In "Step 2: Delhivery Order Details", paste:

     -> Result: Red alert: "DISCOUNT_APPROVAL_REQUIRED: Applied discount of 25% exceeds standard threshold (20%)."
  8. ATTEMPT B: Enter the correct CEO Admin PIN:
       9876
  9. Click "Confirm & Dispatch Order".
Expected Result:
  - Order dispatches successfully with "admin_override: true" flagged in database!
  - Green success receipt displays final discounted price: ₹4,499.


--- TEST CASE 1.3: Duplicate Racket Dispatch Prevention (Strict 1:1 Mapping) ---
Goal: Verify system rejects dispatching a racket that has already been dispatched.
Steps:
  1. In "Step 1: Physical Barcode", paste an ALREADY SOLD barcode:
       TS-ORCE-02-SEED-001
  2. Paste tracking ID:
       DLHV-TEST-1003
  3. Click "Confirm & Dispatch Order".
Expected Result:
  - Rejection with 409 Conflict:
    "CONFLICT: This racket has already been sold on Order #ORD-2026-1001-RIIA. Strict 1:1 mapping forbids duplicate dispatches."


--- TEST CASE 1.4: Duplicate Delhivery Waybill Prevention ---
Goal: Verify system rejects reusing an existing Delhivery tracking number.
Steps:
  1. In "Step 1: Physical Barcode", paste an IN-STOCK barcode:
       TS-SPIN-03-MV6ZH6AK-IYLB
  2. In "Step 2: Delhivery Order Details", paste an ALREADY MAPPED tracking ID:
       DLHV-BOM-901-7000
  3. Click "Confirm & Dispatch Order".
Expected Result:
  - Rejection with 409 Conflict:
    "CONFLICT: Delhivery tracking number 'DLHV-BOM-901-7000' is already mapped to existing Order #ORD-2026-1001-RIIA."


--- TEST CASE 1.5: Invalid Barcode Handling ---
Goal: Verify error handling when scanning an uncatalogued barcode.
Steps:
  1. Enter barcode:
       TS-UNKNOWN-INVALID-999
Expected Result:
  - Red error message: "Item with barcode 'TS-UNKNOWN-INVALID-999' does not exist in inventory."

Goal: Return a customer order, decommission the damaged sticker, and generate
      a brand new barcode + thermal label for restocking.

--- TEST CASE 3.1: Add Multiple Rackets to 1 Order ID ---
Goal: Dispatch 2 or more physical rackets under a single Delhivery Order ID.
Steps:
  1. Open http://localhost:3000 (Sign in as Worker or CEO)
  2. In "Step 1: Add Rackets to Order", enter the first barcode and click "Add Racket":
       TS-AERO-01-MV6ZH6A0-U1VZ
  3. Verify Racket #1 is added to the order list (Topshot Aero Carbon, ₹4,499).
  4. Notice the badge updates to "1 Added".
  5. In the same input, enter a second barcode and click "Add Racket":
       TS-AERO-01-MV6ZH6A0-1Y2S
  6. Verify Racket #2 appears in the order list.
  7. Confirm the combined Preset Total updates to ₹8,998 (2 Rackets).
  8. In "Step 2: Delhivery Tracking Number", enter:
       DLHV-MULTI-WAYBILL-2001
  9. Click "Fetch Details" to populate buyer information.
  10. In "Step 3: Discount & Approval Rule", select 10% discount.
  11. Click "Confirm & Dispatch Order (2 Rackets)".
Expected Result:
  - Green Success receipt displays: "Order Dispatched Successfully!"
  - Dispatched summary shows 2 units, both barcodes listed, and final combined price.
  - Both rackets are atomically marked as "Sold" in the database under that single Order ID.

================================================================================

Goal: Verify that a Worker can only access dispatching and returns, and is blocked from CEO metrics.
Steps:
  1. Click Sign Out (or clear localStorage) and select "Enter as Warehouse Worker".
  2. Notice the navbar displays: "Worker Station | Unlock CEO".
  3. Notice the "CEO: Metrics & SKUs" tab has a lock badge: "PIN Required".
  4. Click the "CEO: Metrics & SKUs" tab.
Expected Result:
  - CEO Authentication modal pops up: "CEO Authentication - Unlock executive metrics & controls".
  - Worker is blocked from seeing gross revenue, net revenue, margins, SKU price editing, and ledger.
  - Worker CAN freely use "Worker: Scan & Dispatch" and "Worker: Process Return / Refund".

Steps:

       9876
  2. Click "Authenticate as CEO" or "Unlock CEO".
Expected Result:
  - Navbar updates to: "👑 CEO Mode (Full Access)".
  - "CEO: Metrics & SKUs" tab opens immediately displaying:
      * Executive metrics cards (Gross Revenue, Net Revenue, Shipping Cost, Units Sold, Return Rate)
